### Hi, I’m Maria

Front-end engineer and textile artist in the San Francisco Bay Area.
I build interfaces with care, and I come to code from art history, digital art and a decade of asking what technology does to the body, to care and to meaning.

*Cloth and code are both made of patterns.*
